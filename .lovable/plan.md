# Single-Pharmacy Stock Management Dashboard

## What I’ll build
- Replace the blank page with the selected frosted clinical dashboard.
- Brand the workspace for one pharmacy and keep branch management out of scope.
- Add a clear role switcher for Pharmacist, Cashier, and Stock Manager.
- Tailor visible tasks and summaries by role while keeping stock safety alerts prominent.
- Include working inventory search, stock-status filters, pagination, alert actions, and an add-stock dialog.
- Make the dashboard adapt cleanly to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

## Role focus
- **Pharmacist:** medicine availability, prescription queue, expiry and safety alerts.
- **Cashier:** quick stock lookup, price visibility, and sales-ready availability.
- **Stock Manager:** stock value, reorder thresholds, expiring batches, suppliers, and stock intake.

## Technical details
- Build the experience with React state and realistic sample pharmacy data.
- Define the selected color, type, glass, status, and animation tokens in the shared design system.
- Add accessible controls, dialogs, labels, semantic page structure, and route metadata.
- Keep this delivery frontend-only; persistent accounts and saved inventory are not included yet.
